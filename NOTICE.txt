--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Game content
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No Fortnite or Skate 3 files are included in this mod. The setup program builds the Skate 3 data the
mod uses from the player's own copy of Skate 3, on the player's own PC. Fortnite itself is never
modified on disk.
